Key Processes
  • PhotosynthesisPlants absorb CO₂ from atmosphere, convert to organic compounds using light energy.
  • RespirationAll living organisms release CO₂ back into the atmosphere.
  • CombustionBurning fossil fuels and wood releases stored carbon as CO₂.
  • DecompositionDecomposers break down dead matter, releasing CO₂ through respiration.
  • FeedingCarbon passes along food chains when organisms consume each other.
  • FossilisationDead organisms become fossil fuels over millions of years — carbon locked away.
