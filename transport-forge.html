Key terms

Concentration gradient — difference in concentration between two areas.

Partially permeable — allows small molecules (water) through, not large ones.

Turgid — plant cell swollen with water.

Plasmolysed — plant cell that has lost so much water the membrane pulls away from the wall.

Diffusion: high → low concentration, no energy, any molecule.

Osmosis: water only, high → low water potential, through partially permeable membrane, no energy.

Active transport: low → high concentration, requires ATP energy and carrier proteins.
