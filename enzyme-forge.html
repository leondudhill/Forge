Hormones
  • FSHFrom pituitary. Stimulates egg maturation in ovaries. Stimulates oestrogen production.
  • OestrogenFrom ovaries. Repairs uterus lining. Inhibits FSH. Stimulates LH surge.
  • LHFrom pituitary. Surge triggers ovulation (egg release) around day 14.
  • ProgesteroneFrom corpus luteum. Maintains uterus lining after ovulation. Inhibits FSH and LH.
Cycle summary
1
Days 1–5: Menstruation — lining breaks down
2
Days 1–13: FSH → egg matures, oestrogen rises
3
Days 5–13: Oestrogen repairs uterus lining
4
Day ~14: LH surge → ovulation
5
Days 14–28: Progesterone maintains lining
6
Day 28: If no fertilisation → progesterone falls → menstruation
