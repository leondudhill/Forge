Mitosis: 1 division → 2 identical diploid cells (46 chromosomes). Used for growth and repair.

Meiosis: 2 divisions → 4 genetically different haploid cells (23 chromosomes). Used to make gametes (sperm & eggs).

Mitosis stages: Prophase → Metaphase → Anaphase → Telophase
